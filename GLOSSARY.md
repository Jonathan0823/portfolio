# Portfolio

The content model of Eguin's portfolio: the kinds of things a reader sees and how they relate.

## Language

**Role**:
A job or period of study, with a start and end date.
_Avoid_: Position, experience item

**Achievement**:
One result delivered inside a Role, stated with a number or a specific outcome.
_Avoid_: Highlight, bullet, entry

**Project**:
Something with a public repo or package that a reader can open themselves. It may link to the Achievement it grew out of, but never repeats it.
_Avoid_: Work, side project, repo

**Skill**:
A tool or technology Eguin has used for real work, listed under a named group.
_Avoid_: Proficiency, tech stack item

**Milestone**:
A dated moment in Eguin's story, told as narrative. It may link to a Role, Achievement or Project, but never replaces one.
_Avoid_: Event, timeline item, story entry

## Page effects

**Ignite**:
The hero field's boot once the intro cover has fully dissolved: a cold, dim field, a spark, then heat flooding outward until the real field takes over.
_Avoid_: Intro, loading animation, boot

**Drip**:
After the Ignite, lava gathers above the hero headline's red period and drops fall from it to build the period, which then blinks as a pixel caret.
_Avoid_: Typing effect, cursor animation

**Wordmark**:
The word EGUIN standing in the footer field's skyline, made of field cells; it dissolves out around a closing button's logo and back in. On touch it is one step of the footer cycle.
_Avoid_: Footer logo, footer title

**Mould**:
The ghost outline of a contact link's logo in the hero that drops of lava fill, inviting visitors to the links. On a fine pointer it stays part-filled until a link is hovered or focused; on touch a small one fills and cycles by itself.
_Avoid_: Hint, placeholder, footer mold (the field mask that forms the Wordmark)
