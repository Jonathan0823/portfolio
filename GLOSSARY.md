# Portfolio

The content model of Eguin's portfolio: the kinds of things a reader sees and how they relate.

## Language

**Role**:
A job or period of study, with a start and end date.
_Avoid_: Position, experience item

**Achievement**:
One result delivered inside a Role, stated with a number or a specific outcome.
_Avoid_: Highlight, bullet, entry

**Project**:
Something with a public repo or package that a reader can open themselves. It may link to the Achievement it grew out of, but never repeats it.
_Avoid_: Work, side project, repo

**Skill**:
A tool or technology Eguin has used for real work, listed under a named group.
_Avoid_: Proficiency, tech stack item

**Milestone**:
A dated moment in Eguin's story, told as narrative. It may link to a Role, Achievement or Project, but never replaces one.
_Avoid_: Event, timeline item, story entry

## Page effects

**Ignite**:
The hero field's boot once the intro cover has fully dissolved: the whole field pours down from the top, hot edge first, until the real field takes over.
_Avoid_: Intro, loading animation, boot

**Drip**:
After the Ignite, lava gathers above the hero headline's red period and drops fall from it to build the period.
_Avoid_: Typing effect, cursor animation

**Runoff**:
The hero field's own drops, which swell, fall and shrink away above the headline. Not the Drip, which happens once and builds the period.
_Avoid_: Drips, rain

**Boil**:
The footer field's own motion: bubbles swell on its surface and pop, and hot spots rise through it.
_Avoid_: Bubbles effect, footer embers

**Wave**:
The hero's and footer's surfaces moving like water. A hovering mouse just outside a surface presses it in and sweeping along it pushes waves out both ways; on touch, a tap near a surface drops into it, and the page sloshes the water when its scrolling speeds up or stops (a fling landing at the bottom splashes the footer). A hard slosh shakes a drop loose from the hero's edge. The surfaces are the only thing the pointer moves; it leaves no mark inside the lava.
_Avoid_: Ripple, trail, cursor effect

**Wordmark**:
The word EGUIN standing in the footer field's skyline, made of field cells; it dissolves out around a closing button's logo and back in.
_Avoid_: Footer logo, footer title

**Mascots**:
Clawd and Pi, two pixel characters standing on the Wordmark's letter tops. They burst out of the lava when the Wordmark rises and sink with it when it goes, and ride the footer's Wave.
_Avoid_: Cameo, sprites, pets

**Clock**:
The time in Karawang as pixel digits, poured from the hero field. It stands by default; a contact link's logo takes its place while that link is hovered or focused, or for a while after it is tapped.
_Avoid_: Timer, widget, glyph cycle
